LuxePix Third-Party Notices
============================

LuxePix is built with open-source Rust libraries. Their package metadata,
source links, license identifiers, and complete license texts are available
from the corresponding crates published through crates.io and from the
LuxePix source repository's Cargo.lock dependency manifest.

Key runtime libraries include eframe/egui, image, rusqlite/SQLite, rayon,
serde, sha2, ureq/rustls, zip, regex, walkdir, and windows-rs.

Each third-party component remains licensed by its respective copyright
holder under the license declared by that component. This notice does not
change or replace those licenses.
